The QuantForge mark: an ink square holding an equity line that stops at a dashed seal, with the forge spark beyond it. `quantforge-mark-on-light.svg` is for Paper grounds (ink #16181b, lines #f4f3ef, spark #c2461a). `quantforge-mark-on-dark.svg` is for Graphite grounds (square #ebe9e4, lines #0d0f11, spark #f08447). In React, use the `Logo` component, which follows the theme. The wordmark is live text in IBM Plex Sans: "Quant" at 600 in `ink`, "Forge" at 400 in `ink-muted`. Keep clear space of half the mark's width, and don't show the mark smaller than 16px.
